Vijay: Life-History & Success Story Vijay, born on June 22, 1974, in Madras, is a prominent Indian actor, playback singer, and politician. He made his cinematic debut in 1984 and quickly rose to fame with his performances in romantic and action films. Vijay's portrayal of a passionate singer in "Thullatha Manamum Thullum" (1999) and his role as an action hero in "Thirumalai" (2003) solidified his status as a leading actor in Tamil cinema. His transition into politics began with the founding of the Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) in 2009, where he has been active in social welfare and political activism. Vijay's influence extends beyond the screen, as he has become a cultural icon and a voice for Tamil Nadu's aspirations.
